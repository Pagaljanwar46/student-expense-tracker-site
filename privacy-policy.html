Privacy Policy

Student Expense Tracker

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Student Expense Tracker is designed to help students record and review their daily expenses. This policy explains what information the app may collect and how that information is used.

2. Information We Collect

When Google Sign-In is implemented, the app may receive basic account information such as your name, email address, and Google account identifier.

When you record an expense, the app may store the description, amount, date, and other information you enter into the app.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Authenticate users and manage their accounts.
  • Save and display their expense records.
  • Calculate daily spending totals.
  • Maintain and improve the app's functionality.
  • Respond to privacy or support requests.

4. Data Storage and Service Providers

The app is planned to use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore for account authentication and expense storage. These services process information according to their applicable privacy terms and security practices.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the app or disclosed when required by applicable law.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures will be used to protect stored information, including access controls designed to prevent users from accessing other users' expense records. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Expense records and account information may be retained while an account is active. Users may request account and data deletion using the contact information below. Applicable retention requirements may affect deletion.

8. Children's Privacy

Student Expense Tracker is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is legally required.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app's features and data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
